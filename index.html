Our story

Built around a simple idea: know your pharmacist.

Larkspur started in 2014 with one counter, one compounding bench and a belief that healthcare should feel human. No queues behind numbered tickets. No rushing you out the door.

Ten years on, we're still independent, still local, and still the place where the person behind the counter knows which tea you like.
