How we started

One counter. One bench. One good idea.

In 2014, pharmacist Amara Osei opened Larkspur in a former florist's shop with a simple belief: people deserve a pharmacy where someone looks up, says hello, and takes the time to explain.

We built the compounding bench first. Then we added a place to sit, a tea shelf, and later a delivery bike. Every addition answered the same question — what would make this visit easier for the person in front of us?

Today we're a team of six, still independent, still on the same street, and still doing things the slow, careful way when it matters.
